Our story

Cooked over fire, served with heart.

Ember & Oak started with one idea: food tastes better when it’s cooked over real wood. Our chef built the oven by hand, and it hasn’t gone cold since.

We cook what the season gives us, pour drinks we love, and treat every guest like family.
